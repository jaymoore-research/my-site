Cell-type marker recovery above random expectation. The frozen MARKERS-CL v5 panel comprises 1,937 genes assigned to 204 Cell Ontology classes, assembled from PanglaoDB [1], CellMarker 2.0 [2], Human Protein Atlas single-cell expression [3] and pinned brain reference profiles. Source-specific evidence filters and ambiguity rules assign each gene to one class, with at most 20 markers per class. The original panel was restricted to an earlier CTD’s gene list.

Within each CTD, population expression profiles are normalised over its native genes and averaged equally across populations with the same exact cell-type identity, combining organs for evaluation only. For each assessable marker, cell types are ranked by normalised expression. Top-3 recovery is the percentage whose expected cell type ranks among the three highest; ties use the worst rank and zero/flat signals count as misses.

The y-axis shows observed top-3 recovery (%) minus the theoretical random expectation (100 × 3 / number of competing classes). Labels show positive marker tests / assessable marker tests: broad 119/133, medium 250/378, fine 480/935, HCL 331/545 and Descartes 185/365. These denominators count marker genes/tests, not cells.

Each CTD uses its own classes, native genes and available markers. This is an exploratory marker-consistency benchmark, not a matched atlas ranking, permutation test, individual-cell accuracy estimate or validation of tissue assignment. Source independence has not been established.

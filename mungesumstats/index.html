Each mark shows one MungeSumstats commit and its tested BSgenome version. For each file, we take the median of two runs per build. Each mark shows the median across files in that benchmark.

We show trials with two complete runs per build for every input. We exclude a trial when its two baseline runtimes differ by more than 10% of their mean on over a third of its inputs.

Dashed horizontal lines mark targets relative to the first measured baseline for the selected benchmark.

Screening input sets appear once at least ten measured builds meet the reading checks.

The main screening benchmark uses three inputs: the full GCST005538 file and one million rows each from GCST006345 and GCST90000489. The harness runs baseline then candidate on one CPU core and candidate then baseline on a second core in parallel.
